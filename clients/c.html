Everything on one chart

Everything on one chart. The colored lines show each day as a percent of its target, so the dashed 100% line is "hit the target" for all of them. The thick white line is your 7-day average weight, read on the right side in pounds. Triangles along the bottom are training days. Tap a day to see its real numbers, and tap a name below the chart to hide or show that line. Hollow sodium dots are days where some foods had no label, so the real number is higher.

Tape measurements

—

Bare skin, every two weeks, at the same time of day each time. All measurements in inches. Shoulders are measured around, just above the nipples. Arms, thighs and calves are left / right, measured 6 inches from the knee for thighs and calves. Change is from the first entry.

On a phone, swipe the table sideways to see shoulders, neck, arms, thighs and calves.

Each meal with its macros, a total for each day, and a total for each week. Calories and macros are what you entered in the form.
